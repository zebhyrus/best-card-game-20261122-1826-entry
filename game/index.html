EMBER DUEL

A turn-based card duel. 30 life. 3 energy a turn.
Outplay the rival, card by card.

1▲Ember Strike⚔ 3
1●Tide Guard🛡 4
1◆Chain Spark⚔ 2+3

Goal: reduce the rival from 30 life to 0.

Each turn: gain 3 energy, draw 1 card. Cards cost energy to play.

Combos: Chain Spark deals +3 if you already played a card this turn. Shield absorbs damage. Tide Leech heals you.

Controls: click a card, or press 1–7. Space ends your turn. Enter starts, R restarts.

An original game. No accounts, no network, just cards.
